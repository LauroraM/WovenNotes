# WovenNotes

A private companion app for a Vampire: The Masquerade-style larp. It keeps your character's world in one place: in-character threads and boards, a connections graph, notes, a character sheet and more.

**Try it:** [https://lauroram.github.io/WovenNotes/]

## What's in it
- **Threads and boards:** in-character conversations, with each thread simply being the characters in it (doesn't work yet)
- **Connections:** a graph of who knows or owes whom, with clan-coloured nodes and a timeline slider (requires a character sheet)
- **Contacts, boons and notes:** including session notes and vision notes
- **Character sheet:** a sheet builder with Hunger and Humanity trackers
- **Resources, news and search:** a reference library, a news feed, and search across everything
- **Backups:** export and import your data as a file

## Install on your phone
1. Open the link above on your phone.
2. **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
3. **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu, then "Install app".
4. Open it from the home screen icon.

## Privacy
- Everything is stored **only on your own device**, in your browser. There is no server, no account, and the app makes no network requests.
- Nobody else, including me, can see what you write. Your data is also not shared between people or devices.
- You can turn on a **passcode lock with encryption** in Settings. If you forget the passcode and lose the recovery key, your data can't be recovered.
- Use the **export** button in Settings now and then. Clearing your browser data deletes your notes.

## Notes
- The app works offline once it has loaded.
- It's a hobby project made for personal use, so expect rough edges.

## Files
`index.html` is the whole app. `sw.js` and `manifest.json` make it installable and work offline. The two PNGs are the app icons.
